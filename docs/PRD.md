# Product Requirements Document: African Teller

## 1. Project Overview
The project is titled "Design and Implementation of a Digital Platform for African Cultural Heritage" (African Teller). The platform aims to preserve and promote Cameroonian folktales, legends, and traditions through an immersive digital experience.

## 2. Target Audience & Roles
*   **Visitors (Explorers)**: Users who browse stories, scan QR codes, take quizzes, and earn achievements.
*   **Cultural Contributors**: Historians, researchers, and users who create and manage cultural content.
*   **Institution Managers**: Representatives of museums and cultural organizations who manage published content.
*   **Administrators**: Super-users who oversee platform operations, manage users, and approve content.

## 3. Strict Scope Definitions (Anti-Hallucination Guardrails)

### In-Scope Core Features
*   **Interactive Storytelling**: Delivery of cultural content via text, images, audio narration, video, and AI-generated visual content.
*   **QR Code Integration**: System must generate and read QR codes linking physical artifacts in museums to digital stories.
*   **Gamification Engine**: Implementation of points, achievement badges, and user levels to motivate engagement.
*   **Educational Modules**: Post-story quizzes and learning assessments to reinforce cultural knowledge.
*   **Centralized Repository**: Search and discovery features categorized by country, region, and theme.

### Out-of-Scope (Future Perspectives - DO NOT INVENT)
*   Augmented Reality (AR) integration for museum storytelling.
*   Virtual museum tours and live virtual cultural events.
*   Multilingual translation of content into various African languages.
